# Goal

A reusable Backplane KiCad footprint library, published in a repository under BackplaneWorks.

# Parts

- Authored mounting holes, `libraries/Backplane_MountingHole.pretty/`: original geometry with silk and internal copper clearance, not ordered.
- Backplane logos, `libraries/Backplane_Logo.pretty/`: mark and mark + wordmark, front silk, back silk and copper/mask, generated from the brand SVGs, not ordered.
- Shoggoth mascot and badge, `libraries/`: 25, 35 and 50 mm sizes, not ordered.
- Generated resistor, capacitor and existing inductor footprints, `libraries/`: least, nominal and most variants, not ordered.
- Two-layer and four-layer mounting-hole experiments, `verification/holes/`: not ordered.
